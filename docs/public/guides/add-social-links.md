# Add Social Links to Author Profiles (Pro)

This feature requires the Pro version of the PublishPress Authors plugin.

This guide will show you how expand author profiles in [PublishPress Authors](https://publishpress.com/links/authors) with links to social profiles.

The Pro version of [PublishPress Authors](https://publishpress.com/links/authors) has a feature called “Author Fields”. This feature arrives with several default fields for you to use:

- Twitter / X
- Instagram
- LinkedIn
- YouTube
- Facebook
- TikTok

![Social fields](https://docs-media.metaslider.com/publishpress-authors/authors-social-fad7b71dc7.webp)

Follow these steps to add a field to your author profiles:

- Edit the field and change the “Field Status” to “Active”.
- Go to the “Author Profile” area and enter a profile link for your own account. This will show that the fields is working, and also give us an example to test with.

![Author Field for YouTube](https://docs-media.metaslider.com/publishpress-authors/add-profile-link-8054db10c1.webp)

- Go to the “[Author Boxes](https://publishpress.com/links/author-box)” area and edit an [author box](https://publishpress.com/links/author-box).
- Go to the “Author Fields” tab and preview your new field. In this screenshot below, I’ve enabled the YouTube field:

![YouTube field](https://docs-media.metaslider.com/publishpress-authors/field-youtube-d7b80cf4f4.webp)

- You may need to adjust some settings such as the “Color” option to match your design. You can also customize other settings such as prefix, suffix, size, height, and the [icon](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/authors-fields-icons/).

![Color for YouTube icon](https://docs-media.metaslider.com/publishpress-authors/youtube-color-26b646d9f0.webp)
