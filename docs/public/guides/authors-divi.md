# PublishPress Authors and Divi

[PublishPress Authors](https://publishpress.com/links/authors) works well with [the Divi theme.](https://www.elegantthemes.com/gallery/divi/)

There are [multiple options for showing Author profiles on your site](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/display-multiple-authors/). All of these will work with Divi. For example, Option #1 will automatically show authors under your posts.

However, we have made sure that [PublishPress Authors](https://publishpress.com/links/authors) works with inside the main Divi layout builder.

Perhaps the easiest option for the Divi layout builder is Option #2 in the link above.

- Go to Appearance > Widgets.
- Create a new widget region using this Divi box. I called my widget region “Authors Area”.

![Create Widge Area](https://docs-media.metaslider.com/publishpress-authors/create-widge-area-9ed68bccd7.webp)

*Create Widget Area*

- Place the “Authors” widget into your new widget region:

![Place Divi Widget](https://docs-media.metaslider.com/publishpress-authors/place-divi-widget-8b414ba1fc.webp)

*Place Divi Widget*

- Now go to edit a post or page with Divi.
- Choose the “Sidebar” module.

![Insert Module Divi](https://docs-media.metaslider.com/publishpress-authors/insert-module-divi-8d1a1f00ea.webp)

*Insert Module Divi*

- In the “Widget Area” box, choose your new widget region:

![Authors Area Divi](https://docs-media.metaslider.com/publishpress-authors/authors-area-divi-1788f86f66.webp)

*Authors Area Divi*

- The Authors box may not appear initially in the admin area of Divi, but trust us – it will work.
- Move out of the Divi layout builder into the normal WordPress editing screen. Choose your authors, as in this image.

![Divi Authors Box](https://docs-media.metaslider.com/publishpress-authors/divi-authors-box-0054b80ba1.webp)

*Divi Authors Box*

- When you visit the front of your site, the author profiles will be visible:

![Divi Authors](https://docs-media.metaslider.com/publishpress-authors/divi-authors-01757bec44.webp)

*Divi Authors*
