# The Post Author Widget

The [PublishPress Authors](https://publishpress.com/links/authors) plugin provides two widgets.

The “Post Author” widget will display a list of authors for the current post. It is [one of several ways that you can show the authors of a post](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/display-multiple-authors/).

- In your WordPress site, go to Appearance > Widgets.
- Look for the “Post Author” widget.

![Post Author](https://docs-media.metaslider.com/publishpress-authors/post-author-f2457a1ab0.webp)

*Post Author*

- Drag the “Post Author” widget into one of your sidebar areas:

![Show Post Author Widget 1](https://docs-media.metaslider.com/publishpress-authors/show-post-author-widget-1-0868d6da4f.webp)

*Show Post Author Widget 1*

- Inside the widget, you can choose from [different layout options](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/layout/):

![Layout Options Widget](https://docs-media.metaslider.com/publishpress-authors/layout-options-widget-f79675adf6.webp)

*Layout Options Widget*

- This next image shows how your Post Authors widget may appear on the front of your site. This widget will automatically adapt to show the authors for the post shown on the screen.

![Authors Widget](https://docs-media.metaslider.com/publishpress-authors/authors-widget-dc5c51d3e3.webp)
