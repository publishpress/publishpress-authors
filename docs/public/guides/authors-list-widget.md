# The Authors List Widget

The [PublishPress Authors](https://publishpress.com/links/authors) plugin provides two widgets.

The “Authors List” widget will display a list of all the authors on your site. If you want to show the authors of the post being viewed, try [the Post Author widget](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/post-author-widget/).

- In your WordPress site, go to Appearance > Widgets.
- Look for the “Authors List” widget.
- Drag the “Authors List” widget into one of your sidebar areas:

![Authors List Widget Enable](https://docs-media.metaslider.com/publishpress-authors/authors-list-widget-enable-38e84b31b1.webp)

*Authors List Widget Enable*

- Inside the widget, you can choose from [the default layout options](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/layout/) and two custom layouts (Authors Index and Authors Recent). There are also multiple other options for customizing the output of your authors display.

![Authors List Widget Update 1](https://docs-media.metaslider.com/publishpress-authors/authors-list-widget-update-1-ff3d904a8b.webp)

*Authors List Widget Update 1*

- This next image shows how your Authors List widget may appear on the front of your site. This widget will look the same on every page of your site:
